# Privacy Policy for Sproutly

**Last Updated: July 19, 2026**

## Introduction

Sproutly ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our child development milestone tracking application.

## Information We Collect

Sproutly is designed with privacy as a priority. The app stores all data **locally on your device only**.

### Data Stored Locally

- **Child Profile Information**: Name, birth date, premature birth status (if applicable), and gestational age
- **Milestone Records**: Completion status, dates, and optional notes you add
- **App Preferences**: Theme settings and display preferences

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers
- We do **not** use analytics or tracking services
- We do **not** share your data with third parties
- We do **not** require an account or login
- We do **not** access your device's location, contacts, photos, or other personal data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app

## How Your Data is Used

All data you enter into Sproutly remains on your device and is used solely to:

- Display your child's developmental milestones
- Track milestone completion over time
- Provide age-appropriate guidance and suggestions
- Show developmental screening reminders based on AAP guidelines

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored locally using iOS's secure storage mechanisms (UserDefaults and SwiftData)
- Data persists on your device until you delete the app
- No data is backed up to our servers (though it may be included in your iCloud backup if you have that enabled in iOS settings)

## Your Control Over Data

You have complete control over your data:

- **View**: All data is visible within the app
- **Edit**: You can modify any information at any time
- **Delete**: Deleting the app removes all stored data from your device
- **Reset**: You can reset your profile from the Settings screen within the app

## Children's Privacy

Sproutly is designed for parents and caregivers to track their children's development. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is intended for use by adults aged 18 and over.

## Medical Disclaimer

Sproutly is an educational tool for tracking developmental milestones. It is **not** a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your pediatrician with questions about your child's health and development.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## International Users

Sproutly is available worldwide. Since all data is stored locally on your device, no international data transfers occur.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information under laws such as GDPR (Europe) or CCPA (California). Since Sproutly does not collect or transmit your data, these rights are inherently protected:

- **Right to Access**: All your data is accessible within the app
- **Right to Deletion**: Delete the app to remove all data
- **Right to Portability**: Your data exists only on your device
- **Right to Opt-Out**: No data collection occurs, so no opt-out is necessary

## Third-Party Services

Sproutly does **not** integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.
